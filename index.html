CompressBox

Compress batches of images and videos locally on your Mac. Choose your settings, review the result, and export when it looks right.

  • Batch image compression
  • MP4, MOV, and M4V video
  • Files stay on your Mac

Image organization

ImageRenamer

Turn a folder of uneven filenames into an ordered sequence. Reorder images, preview the names, and export renamed copies.

  • Prefix, padded number, and suffix
  • Duplicate detection by file content
  • Original files stay unchanged

Remote files

CloudBridge

Connect to an SFTP server you control. Browse remote folders, preview with Quick Look, and download files or folders to your Mac.

  • Direct SSH/SFTP connection
  • Remote file browsing and search
  • Download controls and history
